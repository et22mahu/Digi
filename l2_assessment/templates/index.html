Mythical Creature of the Week: Drakons

Drakons are creatures from Greek Mythology with enormous serpentine bodies, often with crests, beards or multiple heads. They lacked wings and typically moved by slithering. They primarily live in deep caverns, sacred groves and remote springs. They are often sleepless and possess a gaze that can paralyze victims.
